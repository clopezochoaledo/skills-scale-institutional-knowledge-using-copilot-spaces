# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, develop test strategies, and validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria before release. They ensure product reliability and catch defects early in the development cycle.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with feature acceptance criteria
- Design test cases covering functional, performance, security, and usability aspects
- Execute manual and automated testing during development
- Identify defects, collaborate with developers on prioritization and fixes
- Validate that Definition of Done quality standards are met
- Participate in release readiness reviews and gate decisions
- Report quality metrics and trends to the team and leadership

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable releases that meet or exceed customer expectations
- Catch defects early in development to reduce production issues and rework
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage and system observability
- Build team confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning (testing approach and acceptance criteria validation)
- Daily standups (test progress, blocking issues, and defect status)
- PR/code review comments (test coverage and quality feedback)
- QA sign-off meetings before release
- Quality metrics reports to Project Manager and Product Manager

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, conduct design reviews, and mitigate technical risks to ensure scalability, maintainability, and alignment with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions aligned with project goals, constraints, and organizational strategy
- Conduct architecture and code design reviews to ensure quality and consistency
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and guide technical decisions through collaboration
- Ensure adherence to coding standards, best practices, and architectural patterns
- Advise on performance, security, and scalability concerns
- Make final decisions on technology choices and architectural trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions that scale
- Reduce technical debt and rework through proactive design
- Build scalable, secure, and resilient systems
- Foster technical excellence and continuous learning within the team

### Typical Communication
- Technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) and design documentation
- Code reviews with technical feedback and mentoring
- Risk register contributions and technical risk mitigation planning
- Stakeholder briefings on technical strategy and trade-offs

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic direction, approve key decisions, and ensure alignment between projects and organizational objectives. They remove organizational barriers and provide executive oversight.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic objectives and success metrics aligned with business goals
- Approve project charter, scope, and major changes
- Make trade-off decisions on budget, timeline, and scope
- Provide executive oversight and governance throughout the project lifecycle
- Communicate project importance and vision to the broader organization
- Escalate blockers and remove organizational barriers that impede progress
- Approve go/no-go decisions for major releases and milestones

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value and ROI
- Maintain executive alignment and organizational support for the project
- Enable quick resolution of organizational and resource barriers
- Create conditions for team success

### Typical Communication
- Bi-weekly or monthly status updates tailored to strategic priorities
- Milestone approvals and gate reviews at key decision points
- Escalation of critical organizational or resource issues
- Stakeholder briefings and executive communication
- Go/no-go decisions for major releases

---

## Scrum Master/Team Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters remove impediments, facilitate team ceremonies, and coach teams on agile practices to optimize delivery flow and build high-performing, self-organizing teams.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and remove blockers that impede progress
- Coach team members on agile principles, practices, and continuous improvement
- Maintain and update sprint board and project metrics (velocity, burndown, etc.)
- Escalate systemic issues to project leadership
- Foster psychological safety, trust, and team collaboration
- Track action items and ensure follow-up on improvements
- Support team in process refinement and adaptation

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and delivery predictability
- Build high-performing, self-organizing, collaborative teams
- Improve processes, team effectiveness, and satisfaction
- Remove obstacles to team success

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching conversations with team members
- Progress updates and metrics reporting to Project Manager
- Action item tracking and follow-up between retrospectives
- Process improvement discussions and experiments

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear requirements and user stories, bridging the gap between stakeholders and delivery teams. They ensure requirements are unambiguous and aligned with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders through interviews and workshops
- Translate business needs into user stories with clear, testable acceptance criteria
- Clarify ambiguous requirements and resolve conflicts between stakeholder requests
- Participate in backlog refinement and prioritization discussions
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business needs through UAT and feedback loops
- Document and communicate process flows, dependencies, and business logic
- Act as liaison between stakeholders and the delivery team
- Support release communication and user documentation

### Goals
- Ensure delivery aligns with business objectives and customer needs
- Reduce rework and scope creep caused by unclear or changing requirements
- Enable faster decision-making through clear, comprehensive documentation
- Build shared understanding between business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirements gathering sessions
- User story workshops and refinement meetings with the team
- Backlog refinement and prioritization discussions
- Acceptance testing and validation with stakeholders
- Q&A during development and code reviews on requirement clarity

## How These Personas Interact

### Key Collaboration Points

1. **Product Manager + Business Analyst**: Align business goals with user needs and stakeholder feedback; translate strategy into prioritized backlog

2. **Technical Lead + Developers**: Guide implementation and architectural decisions; conduct design reviews; mentor on best practices

3. **QA Lead + Developers**: Collaborate on acceptance criteria clarity; review test coverage; identify defects and blockers

4. **Scrum Master + All Roles**: Facilitate efficient ceremonies; remove blockers; coach teams; foster collaboration

5. **Project Manager + Sponsor**: Communicate status, escalate risks and blockers; resolve organizational barriers; maintain alignment

6. **Technical Lead + QA Lead**: Ensure architectural consistency; define quality standards; assess testing implications of technical decisions

7. **Business Analyst + QA Lead**: Validate acceptance criteria are testable; define test scenarios; confirm user story completeness

8. **Product Manager + Sponsor**: Align on strategic priorities; communicate market feedback; adjust roadmap based on business context

### Examples of Cross-Functional Scenarios

**Scenario: Unclear Requirements Impact Development**
- Developer raises question in standup
- Scrum Master escalates to Business Analyst
- Business Analyst clarifies with Product Manager and Stakeholders if needed
- Product Manager updates backlog and acceptance criteria
- Team continues with confidence

**Scenario: Technical Complexity Threatens Timeline**
- Technical Lead identifies risk in architecture review
- Technical Lead escalates to Project Manager
- Project Manager assesses impact with QA Lead and Scrum Master
- Project Manager escalates to Sponsor if timeline/scope trade-off needed
- Team executes mitigation plan agreed by leadership

**Scenario: Quality Issues Discovered Late**
- QA Lead identifies blocking defects
- QA Lead escalates to Technical Lead and Project Manager
- Technical Lead triages severity and feasibility of fix
- Project Manager escalates to Sponsor for go/no-go decision
- Developers prioritize fix; QA Lead reverifies
- Product Manager assesses business impact
